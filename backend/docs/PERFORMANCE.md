## What is slow, and why

- **`year-report` — 580 ms** (Reports → attendance by batch, academic year). Aggregates a year of attendance (~800k rows) It aggregates rather than looks up, so its cost is proportional to the range asked for, not to the page size.
- **`low-attendance` — 636 ms** (Attendance → low attendance list). The heaviest screen in the product It aggregates rather than looks up, so its cost is proportional to the range asked for, not to the page size.
